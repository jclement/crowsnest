## Checked and found sound, within this static review

- The pure transition validator rejects reopening terminal states and transitions back to queued. Most mutations reload under one service mutex; R2-C08 concerns the same-state exception and side effects that escape that protection, not an unrestricted terminal-to-running transition.
- Ordinary question settlement is conditional, delivery claims prevent concurrent confirmed typed sends, and the revised adapter preserves uncertainty. Short typed outcomes explicitly distinguish expiration/cancellation from consent; long answers require full retrieval.
- `termtext` removes terminal controls/format/private-use characters, flattens whitespace and quotes agent-authored title fragments. No additional concrete defect found in its reviewed callers.
- Reviewed `internal/voice` client, service, text/audio cache, talk presence, SAY parser and reply watcher. The text cache and presence are bounded; per-session request admission is locked, STT is bounded, upstream error bodies are not surfaced as API errors, and incomplete audio streams are not cached as complete. No additional confirmed finding asserted here; concurrent uncached TTS requests are not coalesced, so the cache is not a guarantee of one upstream charge per text.
- Reviewed `internal/journal` and its store queries: nil journals are safe, pagination fetches one extra row, values are parameterized, and write failures are logged without undoing caller effects.
- Poker's pure hold/gate logic excludes working/dialog/draft states, tracks settled-state identity, and serializes checks. Its delivered markers persist; its pending reasons do not (R2-C13).

## Limits and not reviewed

- No runtime, race-detector, integration, browser, or live Claude/herdr validation was performed. Findings are concrete source-level control-flow and interleaving analyses; reported green gates do not establish these interleavings are covered.
- Reviewed relevant command wiring for questions/jobs/pokes and the new agent protocol. This was not a complete new security audit, frontend review, deployment review, or independent verification of ElevenLabs' current external API contract.
- Same-UID token theft is explicitly outside the identity boundary documented by the project; R2-C15 concerns the registry's own replacement semantics, not that accepted limitation.
- Residual R1b-C06 is counted only in the old-findings verdict table. New findings are distinct from that residual and from one another; several amplify the same visible symptom of an unfinished idle job.
