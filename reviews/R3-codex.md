## Coverage and limits

- Traced claims through claim/advance/holds/finish, stale resolution, launch, held brief, dispatch, restart, retries, nudges, Stop, and terminal invalidation. Claim generation changes prevent old completions from committing after cancellation. I found no concrete reverse lock ordering between the jobs mutex and identity-registry mutex in these paths; registry operations do not call back into jobs.
- Traced delegation limits, inherited settings, approval creation and settlement, startup reconciliation, and ordinary card cancellation. Manual cancellation of an approval-held job works; its orphaned open question is an acknowledged existing limitation, not a new finding here. The missing-link failure in R3-C02 is distinct because it has no usable approval decision path.
- Reviewed file-based briefs: text is written under the per-job directory and a short pointer is typed; Claude has SessionStart/job_get fallback, and Codex's file includes the protocol. No additional concrete content-loss finding in that mechanism; R3-C04 concerns how delivery completion changes state. Filesystem permissions do not isolate agents sharing one UID, an explicitly accepted boundary.
- Checked the changed acknowledgement, token replacement, delivery cursor, poker persistence and replay code. Reviewed selected regression-test scenarios as source only. The cancellation tests cover cancellation during observation and between agent start and briefing; they do not establish ordering between the final holds check and the subsequent prompt effect.
- This was not another full frontend, deployment, voice, or security audit. Newly identified implementation defects referenced by a partly-fixed verdict appear once in the new-findings count; R2-C08's residual appears only in its verdict row.

Counts: previous findings — 14 fixed, 3 partly, 0 not fixed. New findings — 0 critical, 4 high, 4 medium, 0 low.

The “never silently stops, never loops forever” promise is not yet sound for Claude agents started by crowsnest, despite substantial improvement in the ordinary paths. The single biggest remaining risk is interrupted restart recovery leaving a live idle job marked exited, with its claim gone and every recovery/notification path deferring to work that nobody owns.
