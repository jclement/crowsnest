# Update the board and notes

Once an hour, keep your own notes current so a compaction or a new conversation loses nothing.

1. Open `notes.md` in your working directory (create it if missing). Keep three short sections:
   **Open work** (each live job: id, title, state, what it waits on), **Waiting on the human**
   (questions asked and not answered), **Standing instructions** (anything the human told you to
   keep doing).
2. Bring each section up to date from what you know now; drop what is finished.
3. Do not message the human. Reply `noop` when done.
